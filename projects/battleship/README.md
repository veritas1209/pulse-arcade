# Battleship

Independent Pulse Arcade game: https://222.96.173.194/games/battleship/

A shared30×30 naval battlefield with three islands, alternating team turns, seven independently controlled ships per team, AP movement, reconnaissance fog and authoritative online1v1 or fair PvE. This replaces the previous stationary10×10 game.

Fleet: carrier×1 HP1000/AP4; destroyer×3 HP500/AP4; battleship×3 HP800/AP3. Both teams' first turn forbids all attacks, including automatic aircraft strikes. Local vision is21 cells; a failed radar reduces it to9. Battleship range isdiameter25/radius12.5. Each movement cell/attack/sortie/damage-control action costs1 AP. Carrier sortie is once perturn beforemovement, reveals21 cells until next ownturnend, and strikes the nearest detected enemy once for200 damage. It can move with remainingAP afterlaunch. Destroyerescort protects friendlyships in21cells with a single50% missile interception roll. Carrier defense retains33%cancel, then33%half, unless its AA system isdisabled. Engine/weapon/flightdeck failure disables move/fire/sortie respectively. Damagecontrol restores50HP and partial hardware, suppressesfire, costs1AP, has2stores perbattle/onceperturn; it never rebuildsdestroyedparts. Zero fleetAP automaticallyadvances theturn; actionlessfleets skip; bothincapablefleets draw.

Select a ship and right-click a water cell to move immediately (touch: tap water). Right/middle drag pans the camera. Attack/recon/repair use the bottom actions and confirmation. Unsuppressed fire applies5%missingHP perownturn start, minimum5/maximum50; damage control stops it until another positive hit. Red tiles remember only previously observed contacts; scouting an empty location clears them. Unknown occupancy never triggers a location-revealing rejection: movement spends its public plannedAP and stops upon legitimate new passive contact. Click a visible enemy to inspect its remaining HP and current damaged3D model on the right; it closes when sight is lost. Only carriers have the recon menu. Selected cells keep a bright yellow square. Drag to orbit, right/middle-drag or two-finger drag to pan, wheel/pinch to zoom. The camera stays where you put it during selection, movement and attacks. Zoom along the current viewing direction; Home restores the map view. World and enemy-preview wheel/pinch zoom translate the current view, with a world-space travel limit fitted to the map/model. Swept geometry protection remains; right/middle drag pans either view. The preview supports full model orbit. Before departure, select each ship and place it anywhere in your opposite-corner 7×7 deployment zone; R rotates it45°. Online: create or join a room, place fleets and both ready, refresh reconnects your seat, mutual rematch starts a new battle.

Development: install dependencies with pnpm/npm in a fresh checkout. This workstation uses a node_modules junction to the existing Screw Harbor dependency tree.
